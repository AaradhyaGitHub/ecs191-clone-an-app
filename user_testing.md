# User Testing Report

This report covers the results of my user testing sessions. 

## Individual Tester Results

### Tester 1 

**5-second test:**
The user was able to quickly understand the main pain point the app was trying to solve. They passed the 5-second test.

**Where they got stuck during the task:**
They did not get stuck during the testing process and were able to go through the core loop with ease. 
However, I saw a little bit of pause and hesitation when they saw all the parking spots displayed on the screen. 
They asked what the multiple tags at the top of the app, like Commuter and Resident, did, and suggested maybe adding a pop-up of some sort.

**Debrief answers:**
- 2/5 where 1 is easy to use, 5 is very difficult to use
- Thing to change: Information on the map legend or get rid of it, Placement of the navigate button.

## Patterns Across Users

Both testers passed the 5-second test and understood the purpose of the app right away, and neither got stuck in the core loop. 
The main pattern I have seen so far is a slowing down, and a little bit of having to look around, once the user reached the parking lot screen and clicked on a lot or spot. 
Tester 1 paused when they saw all the spots and tags displayed at once, and Tester 2 felt uneasy clicking on an individual spot. 
It is less intuitive what the options are after the user clicks on a parking spot.

A second pattern is that the navigate feature is not obvious enough. Tester 2 had a hard time finding the navigate button, and noted that not everyone will recognize the arrow icon.

---

## What I Would Change

1. **Remove the clutter that acts as a legend.** The app is straightforward, and the parking spots themselves already have information on who can park there, so I will remove the Commuter and Resident tags at the top.
2. **Remove the individual spot click within a lot.** Since it is not accurate spot information, it is not worth keeping and it slows users down.
3. **Make the navigate button easier to reach and more pronounced.** I will make it larger and easier to find, and not rely only on the arrow icon.
